## Key Insights

1. **CoreExpr infrastructure is solid** - The desugarer, eval_core, and all supporting code work correctly. The integration was the problem, not the implementation.

3. **Testing reveals architecture** - The new test suites clearly show that CoreExpr works in isolation, proving the integration point was wrong.

4. **Separate macro crate makes sense** - Would cleanly solve the integration issues and enable flexible pipeline assembly.

5. **Don't delete working code** - Keeping CoreExpr infrastructure (even disabled) was the right call. It's tested, working, and ready to integrate properly.
